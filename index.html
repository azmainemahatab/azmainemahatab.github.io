Who I Am

About Me

Academic Identity

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Health Science at the University of the People and a B.Sc. (Honours) programme in Botany at Gurudayal Government College under Bangladesh's National University. I have completed the Associate of Science in Health Science at the University of the People, graduating with High Honors and a 3.85/4.00 GPA. My academic focus connects health science, botany, and public health, especially the relationships between environmental conditions, plant life, and human health.

Academic Direction

I aim to strengthen my foundation in health science, botany, and public health through further study, structured research training, and evidence-based academic work. I am especially interested in interdisciplinary approaches that connect environmental conditions, plant–health relationships, and population health.

Research & Academic Collaboration

I am keen to build practical experience through research internships, academic projects, and collaboration with researchers and institutions. My long-term goal is to contribute to research and knowledge-sharing initiatives at the intersection of health, environment, and science. I welcome relevant academic connections and collaborative opportunities that support learning, research development, and evidence-based solutions to public health challenges.
