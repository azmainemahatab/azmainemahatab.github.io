Who I Am

About Me

Introduction

Hi, I'm Mahatab Azmaine Refat 👋 — an aspiring public health researcher with a unique academic background that bridges plant science and human health. I believe that meaningful health solutions start with understanding the environment we live in, and this portfolio reflects my journey across academics, research, and hands-on learning.

Academic Background

I am currently pursuing a dual academic path: an Associate & Bachelor's degree in Health Science from University of the People, and a Bachelor's degree in Botany from Gurudayal College, Kishoreganj, under the National University of Bangladesh. This combination has given me a strong foundation in both biological sciences and public health, allowing me to see health challenges through an environmental lens.

Professional Interests

I'm drawn to the intersection of Environmental Health and Public Health — understanding how ecosystems, plant life, and environmental factors shape community health outcomes. I'm particularly interested in work that connects scientific research with real-world public health practice.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in environmental health, plant-based/ecological factors affecting human health, and public health research methods. I'm especially curious about how environmental science can inform preventive health strategies in underserved communities.

Career Goals

In the short term, my focus is on completing my academic degrees successfully while actively seeking internship and research assistant opportunities to build hands-on experience. In the long run, I aim to pursue a Master's degree (MPH/MS) and build a career in Public Health / Environmental Health, working at the intersection of research and community impact.
