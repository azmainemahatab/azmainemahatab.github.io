## About

I am building an interdisciplinary academic profile at the intersection of **Health Science, Botany, Public Health, and Environmental Health**. I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Health Science at the University of the People and an Honours degree in Botany at Gurudayal Government College under Bangladesh's National University. I have completed an Associate of Science in Health Science at the University of the People, graduating with **High Honors and a GPA of 3.85/4.00**.

My academic interests include public and environmental health, plant–health relationships, literature review, evidence synthesis, scientific communication, and introductory health-data analysis. I am interested in further study, scholarship opportunities, research internships, and academic collaboration.
